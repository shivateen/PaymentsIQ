Capabilities

How it works

Three agents, one decision

The agents run on submission and again each morning before the payment cut-off. Nothing is written back to a source system — PaymentsIQ reads, benchmarks and recommends; a human certifies.

In the demo queue, three claims carry exceptions worth $1.50M. The largest is a regional linehaul rate billed at $2.68 / mile against a contracted $2.08 — the third consecutive period with the same pattern.

Retrieval Agent Pulls the claim pack, purchase order, rate schedule and prior payment history from the connected systems.
Validation Agent Runs the 12-point check suite and benchmarks every line item against maintained norms and comparable claims.
Communication Agent Drafts the vendor query where evidence is missing, and routes the claim to the right approver.
Analyst decision Certify in full, part-certify and withhold the disputed value, raise a query, or reject — with the reasoning already assembled.
